Cancellation & Refund Policy • Last Updated: August 2026

Thank you for choosing AndolaLabs MetroNome.

1. Digital In-App Purchases

All premium upgrades, subscriptions, and one-time unlock purchases for AndolaLabs MetroNome on Android are processed securely via Google Play Billing.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time directly through the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Once cancelled, your subscription features remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

3. Refund Policy

  • Purchases via Google Play: If you made an in-app purchase by mistake or are unsatisfied with the purchase, you can request a full refund directly through Google Play within 48 hours of purchase via Google's refund portal: https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.
  • Direct Support Requests: For refund inquiries after 48 hours, or technical defects where features failed to unlock, please email us with your Google Play Order Number (GPA transaction ID) at: support@andolalabs.com. Valid claims are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and processed within 5–7 business days.
